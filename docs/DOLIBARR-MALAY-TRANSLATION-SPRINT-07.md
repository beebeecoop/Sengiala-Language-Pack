# Dolibarr Malay Translation Sprint 07

## Purpose

Sprint 07 expands the Bahasa Melayu Malaysia language pack for point of sale, cash desk, website, website builder, online payment, PayPal, Stripe, and bank transfer payment workflows.

This sprint builds on Sprint 01 to Sprint 06 terminology decisions and must preserve the approved controlled terminology.

## Scope

Sprint 07 focuses on:

- POS
- Cash desk
- Website
- Website builder
- E-commerce interface
- Online payment
- PayPal
- Stripe
- Bank transfer payment

## Target Files

- `lang/ms_MY/cashdesk.lang`
- `lang/ms_MY/pos.lang`
- `lang/ms_MY/website.lang`
- `lang/ms_MY/websitebuilder.lang`
- `lang/ms_MY/stripe.lang`
- `lang/ms_MY/paypal.lang`
- `lang/ms_MY/paymentbybanktransfer.lang`
